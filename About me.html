about me

Hi, I'm Julia! I'm a senior at Hunter College, majoring in Clinical Psychology with a minor in Media Studies. I was born and raised in the Philippines and now live in New York City. My interests stretch across psychology, art, and digital media, and I enjoy finding ways to bring them together.

Studying psychology makes me curious about how people think, feel, and interpret the world. Through media studies, I get to explore how images, colors, and design can shape those interpretations. This portfolio brings together my experiments with photography, illustration, animation, and web design as I learn to turn ideas into something visual.

Outside of school, you'll usually find me figure skating, watching anime, playing Genshin Impact, or grabbing matcha. I'm drawn to playful details and designs that feel personal-like the scrapbooking, handwritten notes, and little keepsakes that inspired this website. Take a look around and explore what I've been making!
