Creative
Statement

Julia Sta. Maria is an emerging mixed-media artist born and raised in the Philippines. Growing up in a culturally rich country exposed her to different forms of art, sparking a curiosity that makes working exclusively in one medium feel limiting. She is interested in combining photography, digital illustration, and paint to turn familiar scenes into places where unexpected stories unfold. Digitally illustrated vines might climb a photographed New York City building, a drawn figure might peek around a real doorway, or streaks of paint might spill beyond the edges of a printed photograph. Lines, textures, and imagined elements interact with the photographed scene, inviting viewers to discover something unexpected within an ordinary setting.

A self-described jack of all trades, Julia approaches art through experimentation, moving between tools and mediums to discover what each can bring to an idea. She is drawn to the contrast between the stillness of a photograph and the movement of a drawn or painted mark. A small addition can suggest a character, shift the mood, or introduce a story that the original image leaves untold. Her developing practice makes room for playful combinations and unexpected results, allowing each piece to take shape through the process of making. She hopes to create work that rewards curiosity, encouraging viewers to look closer and imagine where the scene might lead.
